Cosmetic

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry improves the look of your teeth, gums and smile. Stained, worn, discoloured, broken, misaligned or chipped teeth and gaps can all be corrected, improving the health and function of your teeth as well as their appearance.

What it includes

Ceramic and invisible braces, composite and porcelain veneers, crowns, gum treatment, composite fillings, whitening and bleaching, and dental implants. We work with you on a plan that is right for you and economical, including complete smile makeovers and pre-wedding makeovers in a short time.
