Exceptional oral care, held to high standards

Veeyes Dental Care is one of the leading dental hospitals in Tamil Nadu, with specialists covering every dental need in a single facility in Coimbatore and Tiruppur.

We aim for dental health free from pain and complications, for people from all walks of life. Our dentists keep bringing the best of modern dentistry into everyday practice, we run dental awareness camps across both districts, and we treat overseas patients who fly in every year for their care.

Patients regularly travel to us from Sathyamangalam, Annur, Karamadai, Mettupalayam, Erode, Ooty and Salem.

Treatments overview

Straighten

  • Teeth AlignmentMetal, ceramic and lingual braces, aligners and early orthodontics.
  • InvisalignClear, removable aligners planned around your teeth.

Replace & rebuild

  • Teeth ReplacementImplants, veneers, crowns, inlays and onlays.
  • Teeth in an HourPermanent implant teeth fixed in a single visit.
  • Full Mouth RehabilitationRebuilding function and appearance across the whole mouth.
  • Restorative DentistryFillings, crowns and root canal treatment that restore function.
  • Biomimetic DentistryMinimally invasive repairs that mimic natural teeth.
  • Tooth Fracture ManagementRepairing chipped, cracked and split teeth.

Cosmetic

  • Cosmetic DentistrySmile makeovers, veneers, bonding and whitening.
  • Smile DesigningPlanning shape, contour and colour for your face.
  • Teeth WhiteningHome and in-clinic bleaching for a brighter shade.
  • Laser DentistryGentler treatment for teeth and gums with dental lasers.

Everyday & family care

  • Pain ManagementRoot canals, extractions and wisdom tooth removal, without the pain.
  • Preventive DentistryCheck-ups, cleaning, fluoride and sealants.
  • Kids & Family DentistryTeddy Teeth, our child-friendly clinic, and care for every age.
  • Geriatric DentistryBPS dentures, implants and gentle care for older patients.
  • Gum TreatmentCleaning, root planing and periodontal surgery.

Diagnostics & technology

  • Digital DentistryCBCT, intra-oral scanning, digital smile design and CAD/CAM.

Why Veeyes

Why patients choose us

The reasons patients give for coming back to us, and for bringing their families.

  • A treatment room at Veeyes

  • Quality treatment

    A professional, friendly environment is one of the main reasons our patients keep coming back.

  • Aesthetics and function

    Eating, speaking, laughing: it all happens in your mouth, and your smile is where first impressions are made. We plan for both looks and function.

  • Multi-speciality under one roof

    Implants, dentures, veneers, simple and complex extractions, Invisalign and cosmetic work, handled by specialists in a single facility.

  • State-of-the-art equipment

    3D CBCT imaging, a Trios 3D intra-oral scanner, dental lasers, microscopic endodontics and a piezo bone-surgery unit.

  • Patient management

    Civility, respect, patience and kindness. Our dentists answer every question, however simple, and explain each plan until you fully understand it.

  • Digital dentistry

    Digital records, RVG, OPG, lateral cephalograms and CBCT for diagnosis, plus digital smile design, digital impressions and CAD/CAM crowns.

  • Five-step sterilisation

    Trained dental assistants follow a five-step sterilisation protocol for every instrument, chair and operatory, in line with international standards.
